ACME Corporation - Employee Handbook (2026)

Section 1: Remote Work
Employees are allowed to work remotely up to 3 days a week. 
Tuesday and Thursday are mandatory in-office days for all engineering staff.

Section 2: Equipment
The company provides a $1000 stipend for home office equipment. 
Purchases must be approved by the IT department before reimbursement.

Section 3: Expenses
All travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the trip. 
Receipts are required for any expense over $25.
